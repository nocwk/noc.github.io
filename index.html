02.
Dual-Homed Edge & eBGP Peering

Architected a dual-homed enterprise edge network utilizing external BGP (eBGP) for redundant ISP connectivity, manipulating route attributes to engineer precise inbound and outbound traffic flows.

03.
Inter-VLAN Departmental Segmentation (ROAS & Extended ACLs)
