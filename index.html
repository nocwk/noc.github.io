Dual-Homed Edge with BGP Traffic Engineering

Segmentation & Router-on-a-Stick

Layer 2 Resilience & Bandwidth Aggregation

Edge Address Translation & Switchport Hardening

Configured dynamic NAT with overload (PAT) for internal client internet access. Enabled Sticky MAC Port Security and DHCP Snooping to eliminate rogue address distribution.
